# TrustLend Regulatory Questions

This file is a question register, not a legal opinion.

Before production, validate:

1. What legal/regulatory structure is appropriate for the intended product?
2. Can the intended lender-specific borrower relationship be supported under the selected model?
3. Which entity originates or facilitates the loan?
4. What KYC requirements apply?
5. What payment/UPI arrangement is permitted?
6. What agreement/e-sign approach is appropriate?
7. What data retention/privacy requirements apply?
8. Whether and through whom applicable credit information may be reported to credit information companies.
9. What recovery/collections activities are permitted and who may perform them?
10. What roles and responsibilities belong to a regulated partner versus TrustLend?

Do not launch real financial activity until these questions have been reviewed by qualified legal/compliance professionals for the final operating model.
