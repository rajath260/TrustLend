# ADR-004: No Proprietary Trust Score

## Status

Accepted

## Decision

TrustLend will maintain a factual platform-specific repayment record instead of creating a proprietary trust score.

## Rationale

The lender's trust decision is part of the use case. TrustLend's value is documentation, repayment tracking and settlement—not deciding who is trustworthy.

## Consequence

User-facing history must describe facts and status, not subjective judgments about a person's character or creditworthiness.
